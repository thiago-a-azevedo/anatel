ANATEL TELECOM QUALITY METHODOLOGY - KNOWLEDGE BASE
(Source: public Anatel documentation on RQUAL, Anatel Consumidor and the Satisfaction and Perceived Quality Survey. Summarized for the Anatel Telecom Advisor.)

1. SERVICES
- SMP (Servico Movel Pessoal): mobile telephony and mobile data.
- SCM (Servico de Comunicacao Multimidia): fixed broadband, including fiber, cable, radio and satellite internet.
- STFC (Servico Telefonico Fixo Comutado): fixed landline telephony.
- SeAC (Servico de Acesso Condicionado): pay TV.

2. RQUAL - QUALITY REGULATION (Regulamento de Qualidade dos Servicos de Telecomunicacoes)
RQUAL replaced the previous per-service quality regulations. It evaluates operators by service, by state (UF) and by municipality.
- IQS (Indice de Qualidade do Servico): score from 0 to 10 combining network indicators, consumer complaints and perceived quality.
- Quality seal (Selo de Qualidade): letters A to E derived from the IQS. A = highest quality, E = lowest quality. Seals are published periodically for each operator, service and geography.
- Network indicators measured under RQUAL include download speed compliance (% of measurements meeting the contracted speed), upload speed compliance, latency (milliseconds, lower is better), jitter (milliseconds, lower is better), packet loss (%, lower is better) and network availability (% uptime, higher is better). Each indicator has a regulatory target; operators are assessed on compliance with the target.

3. ANATEL CONSUMIDOR - COMPLAINTS AND IDA
- Anatel Consumidor is the official channel where consumers register complaints against operators.
- Complaints per 1,000 accesses normalizes complaint volume by customer base. LOWER is better.
- Main complaint reasons typically include billing errors (cobranca indevida), service quality/outages, cancellation difficulties, and customer service.
- Resolution rate (%): share of complaints resolved by the operator. Higher is better.
- Average response time (days): lower is better.
- IDA (Indice de Desempenho no Atendimento): customer-service performance index from 0 to 100 combining complaint rate, resolution and response time. Higher is better.

4. SATISFACTION AND PERCEIVED QUALITY SURVEY - ISG
- Annual survey run by Anatel with consumers of each service.
- ISG (Indice de Satisfacao Geral): overall satisfaction from 0 to 10. Higher is better.
- Dimension scores: functioning (funcionamento), customer service (atendimento telefonico and digital), billing / top-up (cobranca / recarga).

5. CONSOLIDATED OPERATOR SCORE (used by the comparar_operadoras tool)
Score 0-10 = 35% IQS + 30% IDA (rescaled to 0-10) + 35% ISG.

6. INTERPRETATION GUIDELINES
- Always compare operators within the same service and geography.
- A higher IQS, IDA, ISG, availability or speed compliance is better; higher latency, jitter, packet loss or complaints per 1,000 accesses is worse.
- Regional operators (e.g. Brisanet, Algar, Unifique, Vero, Desktop) often score highly in their home regions; national operators (Vivo, Claro, TIM, Oi) have broader coverage.
- Always cite the pillar and the reference period of each figure.

7. OPEN DATA
Anatel publishes datasets on its open data portal (dados abertos, CKAN catalog), including RQUAL panels and consumer complaint panels, as CSV/ZIP files.
